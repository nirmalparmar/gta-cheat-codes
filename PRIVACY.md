# Privacy Policy — Cheat Code for GTA 6

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Cheat Code for GTA 6 ("the app") is an unofficial, fan-made reference app. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive,
Sony Interactive Entertainment or Microsoft.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

## What stays on your device

- Your favorite cheats
- Your chosen console (PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S)
- A cached copy of the cheat list

This information is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.
Deleting the app removes it.

## Network use

To show newly published cheat codes, the app downloads a public file
(`cheats.json`) from GitHub when it opens and when you pull down to refresh. The
request contains no personal information. Like any website, GitHub may log
standard technical details of the request, such as your IP address; see
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
